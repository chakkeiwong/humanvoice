The Pythagorean theorem states that in a right triangle, the sum of the squares of the two legs equals the square of the hypotenuse:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:pythagorean}
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
\end{equation}
where $a$ and $b$ represent the lengths of the two legs, and $c$ represents the length of the hypotenuse. This relationship, referenced by~\ref{eq:pythagorean}, is fundamental to Euclidean geometry and underlies numerous results in mathematics and physics.

## Context

G3 is the reader-session gate that authorizes handoff preparation (WP6). Before proceeding, reader independence must be verified: the reader packet must be immutable and contain everything the reader needs, with no dependency on writer state. Per v1.1 program §5.5:

> Reader packet is immutable. Reader session isolated from writer session. Integration test verifies reader can operate independently.

At prototype scale, "reader" means integration test scope, not recruitment of a real external reader.
